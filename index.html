How to use PinchGS
  1. Start cameraAllow browser camera permission.
  2. Show one handWait for the HUD to say Hand visible.
  3. PinchTouch thumb and index finger to lock a Gaussian cluster.
  4. Fist hammerMake a fist and punch downward to smash Gaussians.
  5. Open palmMove one open hand to rotate the scene.
  6. Two handsMove hands apart or together to zoom.
Game mode rules
Invite friends to tear apart one shared Gaussian scene. Everyone plays on their own computer. Best score wins.
  1. Host uploads a sceneThe shared room keeps one 3DGS scene for all invited players.
  2. Share the invite linkFriends open the link and enter Game mode directly.
  3. 3 tries per playerEach try lasts 15 seconds. Only the highest score is ranked.
  4. Creativity scoreCoverage, rhythm, variety, and spatial play all matter.
