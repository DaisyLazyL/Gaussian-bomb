How to use PinchGS
  1. Start cameraAllow browser camera permission.
  2. Show one handWait for the HUD to say Hand visible.
  3. PinchTouch thumb and index finger to lock a Gaussian cluster.
  4. Fist hammerMake a fist and punch downward to smash Gaussians.
  5. Open palmMove one open hand to rotate the scene.
  6. Two handsMove hands apart or together to zoom.
